Business Strategy Game (BSG)
This project showcases our team's performance in the Business Strategy Game (BSG), a competitive business simulation where teams manage a global athletic footwear company and make strategic decisions across marketing, operations, finance, production, and corporate social responsibility.

🏆 Achievement

Ranked #1 among all competing teams.
Successfully implemented a premium differentiation strategy, resulting in industry-leading financial and operational performance.

Key Accomplishments

Achieved an A+ Credit Rating with strong financial health.
Increased Earnings Per Share (EPS) to 11.80.
Reached a 28.5% Return on Equity (ROE).
Increased Stock Price to $277.73.
Attained a perfect Image Rating of 100.
Built a high-quality product portfolio with 8.9–9.0 S/Q ratings, significantly above the industry average.
Expanded global market share through strategic pricing, targeted marketing, celebrity endorsements, and operational improvements.
Optimized manufacturing by transitioning production capacity from North America to Europe-Africa and Latin America, improving long-term efficiency and competitiveness.
